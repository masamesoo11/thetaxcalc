<a href='https://thetaxcalc-tax-tips.blogspot.com/2026/07/free-2026-tax-calculator-how-much-will.html'>Free 2026 Tax Calculator &#8212; How Much Will You Owe?</a>
</h2>

<span class='author'>

<div class='post-body'>
<p>Wondering how much tax you'll owe in 2026? You're not alone. 
The US tax code is complex, but understanding your tax liability 
doesn't have to be.</p>

<h2>2026 Federal Tax Brackets</h2>
<p>The 2026 tax year uses seven progressive brackets:</p>
<ul>
<li>10% on income up to $11,925</li>
<li>12% on $11,926&#8211;$48,475</li>
<li>22% on $48,476&#8211;$103,350</li>
<li>24% on $103,351&#8211;$197,300</li>
<li>32% on $197,301&#8211;$250,525</li>
<li>35% on $250,526&#8211;$626,350</li>
<li>37% on income over $626,350</li>
</ul>

<p>The standard deduction for 2026 is $16,100 for single filers 
and $32,200 for married couples filing jointly.</p>

<h2>How Much Tax Will You Owe?</h2>
<p>A single filer earning $75,000 in 2026 pays approximately 
 $7,872 in federal income tax (effective rate: 10.5%), plus 
 $5,738 in FICA payroll taxes (7.65%). State tax varies 
from $0 in Texas to $3,200 in California.</p>

<p>To calculate your exact take-home pay, use the 
<a href="https://thetaxcalc.com/paycheck-calculator">free 2026 paycheck calculator</a> 
which covers all 50 US states with accurate federal and state 
tax brackets.</p>

<h2>State Tax Comparison</h2>
<p>Where you live makes a huge difference. A $75,000 earner 
keeps $61,590 in Texas but only $56,390 in Oregon. That's a 
 $5,200 difference per year. See the complete 
<a href="https://thetaxcalc.com/research/2026-state-tax-burden">2026 State Tax Burden Analysis</a> 
for all 50 states.</p>

<h2>Tax-Saving Tips for 2026</h2>
<ul>
<li>Max out your 401(k) &#8212; $23,500 limit saves $5,170 at 22% bracket</li>
<li>Use an HSA &#8212; triple tax advantage, $4,400 limit</li>
<li>Check your W-4 withholding with the 
<a href="https://thetaxcalc.com/irs-withholding-calculator">IRS withholding calculator</a></li>
<li>Consider state tax implications before relocating</li>
</ul>

<p>For more tax guides and calculators, visit 
<a href="https://thetaxcalc.com">TheTaxCalc.com</a> &#8212; 
67+ free tax calculators covering all 50 states.</p>
